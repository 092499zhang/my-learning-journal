My Learning Journey

I’m a self-driven learner exploring web development step by step. This space is where I document what I build, what I struggle with, and how I gradually improve through consistent practice.

Over the past months, I’ve been teaching myself HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, focusing not just on making things work, but on understanding structure, accessibility, and responsive design. This website itself is part of that journey — a small but meaningful exercise in thinking carefully and building intentionally.

Why I started

I actually started learning front‑end development back in 2022. The journey has been slower than I expected. Balancing family life and work means I can’t always move as quickly as I’d like, and sometimes progress feels small. But I keep reminding myself that learning something new takes time. As long as I keep going and stay committed to the dream I started with, I believe the results will come eventually. For now, the most important thing is simply to keep showing up and doing the work.

What I found difficult

One of the hardest parts right now is how long the process can be. Even if I dedicate time to learning, progress still takes patience. Sometimes topics I studied months ago start to fade from memory, simply because I haven’t had the chance to revisit them properly. It’s also not always easy to find long, uninterrupted blocks of time to work on a full project from start to finish. Balancing responsibilities means learning happens in small pieces, and that slow pace can be frustrating at times.

What I want to improve next

Going forward, I want to improve how I learn and organise my progress. I’m experimenting with different approaches, such as using small learning tools for short practice sessions, writing my own notes to reinforce what I study, and breaking larger tasks into smaller, manageable steps. Instead of trying to do everything at once, I’m learning to build projects piece by piece and make steady progress over time.
