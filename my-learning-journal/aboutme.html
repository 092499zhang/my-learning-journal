Hi there! My name is Shu and welcome to my learning journal.

I started learning web development on my own. Over the past few years, I have been studying through online resources and different learning platforms, exploring the world of frontend development step by step. This website is a small space where I document what I learn, what I build, and how I continue improving along the way.

How I stay committed to learning

I believe learning is a lifelong process. There is always something new to explore and improve. Personally, I enjoy discovering new fields and new ideas, which is one of the reasons I became interested in technology.

When studying frontend development, especially the design and creative side of it, I realised how much I enjoy building things and solving problems. Through learning technology, I also feel that I understand more about how our society, our tools, and our digital world continue to evolve.

How I got started

My journey actually started with design. After studying design, I became interested in user experience and began teaching myself UX. Over time, I realised that understanding design alone was not enough for the kind of work I wanted to do.

I started to see how valuable coding knowledge could be when working with digital products. Learning how things work behind the interface felt like a natural next step. That curiosity is what eventually led me to begin learning frontend development and programming.
